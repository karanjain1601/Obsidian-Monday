# Short: Particle Goes Through Wall

**Feeds full video:** I Built a Wall That Particles Go Through
**Duration:** ~45 seconds

---

## Visual Hook (First 3 Seconds)
A glowing quantum wavepacket moving to the right. A tall, thick gray barrier in its path. Classically, the particle can't possibly cross it — its energy is far less than the barrier height. And yet.

## Main Visual Sequence (0:03–0:45)
**Beat 1 (0:03–0:10):** Wavepacket approaches the barrier. The barrier height is labeled: "V₀ = 10 eV." Particle energy labeled: "E = 3 eV." Classical prediction: "TOTAL REFLECTION. NO TRANSMISSION."
**Beat 2 (0:10–0:18):** Packet hits the barrier. Most of the wave is reflected — a large reflected lobe bounces back to the left. But inside the barrier: a decaying exponential — the wavefunction is non-zero inside the forbidden region.
**Beat 3 (0:18–0:26):** Slow motion: the evanescent wave penetrating the barrier, decaying exponentially but not reaching zero. On the FAR side: a small transmitted lobe appears. It exists. "IT WENT THROUGH."
**Beat 4 (0:26–0:32):** Transmission probability shown: "T ≈ 3%." That 3% of the probability amplitude — the square of the wavefunction — made it to the other side. "3% of the time, the particle tunnels."
**Beat 5 (0:32–0:38):** Barrier thickness slider: thicker barrier → T drops exponentially. Thinner barrier → T increases dramatically. Tunneling probability is extremely sensitive to barrier width.
**Beat 6 (0:38–0:45):** "This is quantum tunneling. Without it, the Sun wouldn't shine." Final: "THE PARTICLE WENT THROUGH THE WALL. NO MAGIC. JUST MATH."

## Physics Concept Teased
Quantum tunneling allows a particle to appear on the far side of a classically-forbidden energy barrier — the wavefunction is non-zero inside the barrier, and a finite transmitted amplitude exists regardless of classical energy constraints.
