## Visual Hook (First 3 Seconds)
A glowing Gaussian bell-curve shape moving to the right inside a box. Bright, smooth, beautiful. In the corner: "NORM: 1.000." A quantum probability wavepacket in motion.

## Main Visual Sequence (0:03–0:45)
**Beat 1 (0:03–0:10):** Wavepacket moves right, hits the right wall. Reflects perfectly. Moves left. Norm display: still exactly 1.000. "PROBABILITY IS CONSERVED."
**Beat 2 (0:10–0:18):** During travel, the packet spreads — getting wider and shorter as it propagates. The quantum dispersion of a free particle. "THE UNCERTAINTY PRINCIPLE AT WORK."
**Beat 3 (0:18–0:26):** After several bounces, the spread wavepacket fills more of the box. The packet starts to overlap with its own reflections — INTERFERENCE PATTERNS appearing. Nodes and antinodes visible.
**Beat 4 (0:26–0:32):** Freeze on a beautiful standing-wave-like interference pattern inside the box. "THE PARTICLE IS INTERFERING WITH ITSELF." Norm: 1.000.
**Beat 5 (0:32–0:38):** Time-lapse of many bounces. The wavepacket evolves through a complex, beautiful pattern. Norm stays at 1.000 throughout. The Schrödinger equation is unitary.
**Beat 6 (0:38–0:45):** "This is a quantum particle. Not a marble. Not a wave. Something stranger." Final: "WHERE IS IT ACTUALLY?"

## Physics Concept Teased
A quantum wavepacket is a probability amplitude — it spreads, reflects, and interferes with itself, yet the total probability (the norm) remains exactly 1. What does it mean to know "where" the particle is?
