# Short: The Einstein Ring Forms

**Feeds full video:** I Ray-Traced a Black Hole
**Duration:** ~45 seconds

## Visual Hook (First 3 Seconds)
A black hole at center, perfectly dark, surrounded by a faint glow. A single star is visible at the edge of frame. The star begins to move slowly behind the black hole.

## Main Visual Sequence (0:03–0:45)
**Beat 1 (0:03–0:10):** Star moving slowly toward direct alignment with the black hole. Its light bends slightly, creating a distorted arc on the opposite side. "GRAVITATIONAL LENSING."
**Beat 2 (0:10–0:18):** Star approaching alignment. The arc on the far side grows longer, begins to wrap around the black hole. Two images of the same star now visible on opposite sides.
**Beat 3 (0:18–0:26):** Near-perfect alignment: the star images merge into a nearly complete ring around the black hole. Bright, sharp, unmistakable. "ALMOST."
**Beat 4 (0:26–0:32):** PERFECT alignment: the Einstein Ring snaps into existence — a complete, perfect circle of light enclosing the black hole. Slow motion. The ring pulses with the star's brightness.
**Beat 5 (0:32–0:38):** Star continues moving past alignment — the ring breaks, splits back into two arcs, then to one lensed image, then star moves away normally.
**Beat 6 (0:38–0:45):** Replay the ring formation in slow motion. Freeze at the perfect ring. "ONE STAR. ONE BLACK HOLE. ONE PERFECT CIRCLE." Final: "LIGHT CURVED BY GRAVITY."

## Physics Concept Teased
When a massive object sits perfectly between an observer and a light source, general relativity predicts that gravity bends light into a perfect ring — an Einstein ring — rather than blocking the view.
