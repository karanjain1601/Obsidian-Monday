# Short: 50,000 Fireworks

**Feeds full video:** I Coded Fireworks Physics. My First Attempt Was Confetti.
**Duration:** ~45 seconds

---

## Visual Hook (First 3 Seconds)
Dark night sky. A single click. A point of light launches upward from the bottom. It climbs. Reaches apex. Then: 50,000 glowing particles BURST outward simultaneously.

## Main Visual Sequence (0:03–0:45)
**Beat 1 (0:03–0:08):** Burst in slow motion — particles expanding in a perfect sphere. Counter races to "50,000." Each particle a tiny glowing dot.
**Beat 2 (0:08–0:18):** Drag takes effect — faster outer particles slow more dramatically, shape shifting from sphere to teardrop as gravity pulls all particles down simultaneously.
**Beat 3 (0:18–0:28):** Normal speed. Trails form — each particle leaving a glowing arc drooping from drag and gravity. The firework looks like a weeping willow of light.
**Beat 4 (0:28–0:35):** Second firework launches. Third. Overlapping particle systems. "150,000 PARTICLES SIMULTANEOUSLY." Screen filling with intersecting trails.
**Beat 5 (0:35–0:40):** Grand finale: all remaining fireworks launch at once. Screen fills with light. Then all fade together, leaving drifting particle dust.
**Beat 6 (0:40–0:45):** One last particle drifts across dark sky. "50,000 particles. Each following two equations. DRAG AND GRAVITY." Final: "WHAT WENT WRONG THE FIRST TIME?"

## Physics Concept Teased
How do just two forces — gravity pulling down and drag opposing velocity — applied independently to 50,000 particles create such a recognizable, beautiful firework shape?
