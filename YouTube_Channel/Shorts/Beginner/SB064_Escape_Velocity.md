# Short: Escape Velocity

**Feeds full video:** How Fast Do You Need to Go to Leave a Planet?"
**Duration:** ~45 seconds

---

## Visual Hook (First 3 Seconds)
Earth. A ball launched upward at 5 km/s. It rises, slows, stops, falls back. Text: "5 km/s. COMES BACK." Same ball, 10 km/s. Rises much higher, still falls back. "10 km/s. STILL COMES BACK."

## Main Visual Sequence (0:03–0:45)
**Beat 1 (0:03–0:10):** Multiple launches at increasing speeds: 5, 7, 9, 11 km/s. Each one rising higher before falling. The curve of Earth below each trajectory — showing the planet's curvature matters.
**Beat 2 (0:10–0:18):** At exactly 11.2 km/s: the ball is launched, rises, and keeps rising — never stopping. The trajectory extends off screen. "11.2 km/s. ESCAPE VELOCITY." It never falls back.
**Beat 3 (0:18–0:26):** Energy diagram: at v < v_escape, kinetic energy < gravitational potential energy needed. At v = v_escape: exactly equal. "½mv² = GMm/r → v_escape = √(2GM/r)." Formula shown.
**Beat 4 (0:26–0:32):** Moon comparison: Moon escape velocity = 2.4 km/s. A ball thrown at 3 km/s from the Moon: escapes easily. Same speed from Earth: barely gets to 900 km altitude. "SAME SPEED. DIFFERENT PLANET."
**Beat 5 (0:32–0:38):** Black hole comparison: escape velocity = c. "AT THE EVENT HORIZON, ESCAPE VELOCITY = THE SPEED OF LIGHT. EVEN LIGHT CAN'T ESCAPE." Formula: r_s = 2GM/c².
**Beat 6 (0:38–0:45):** Back to Earth: ball at exactly 11.2 km/s coasting outward forever. "11.2 km/s. THE PRICE OF FREEDOM." Final: "ESCAPE VELOCITY. WHERE DOES INFINITY LIVE?"

## Physics Concept Teased
Escape velocity is the minimum speed at which kinetic energy equals the gravitational binding energy — and the formula v = √(2GM/r) reveals that it depends entirely on the planet's mass and radius, not on the object being launched.
