# Short: Solar Wind Deflects

## Visual Hook (First 3 Seconds)
The Sun blasting streams of charged particles outward. They rush toward Earth. A million miles away: they hit something invisible and curve around it. They never reach the surface. "SOMETHING STOPPED THEM."

## Main Visual Sequence (0:03–0:45)
**Beat 1 (0:03–0:10):** Solar wind: streams of protons and electrons at 400 km/s from the Sun. "400 km/s. CONTINUOUS. ALWAYS." Particles shown as a particle stream rushing from left to right.
**Beat 2 (0:10–0:18):** Earth's magnetic field shown: dipole field lines extending from South Pole to North Pole. The field forms a bubble around Earth — the magnetosphere. "EARTH'S MAGNETIC SHIELD."
**Beat 3 (0:18–0:26):** Solar wind hits the bow shock — the magnetospheric boundary — and is deflected around Earth. Particles following field lines, curving around the magnetosphere. The sunward side is compressed. The nightside: a long magnetotail stretching away.
**Beat 4 (0:26–0:32):** Some particles DO get in: they follow field lines toward the poles (where field lines converge and enter Earth). Result: the auroras. "AURORA BOREALIS: THE SOLAR WIND MADE VISIBLE."
**Beat 5 (0:32–0:38):** Solar storm: dramatically increased particle flux. Magnetosphere compressed further. Auroras visible at lower latitudes. "SOLAR STORM: THE SHIELD STRAINS."
**Beat 6 (0:38–0:45):** "Without the magnetic field, Earth would look like Mars — stripped of its atmosphere over billions of years." Mars shown: no magnetic field, thin atmosphere. Final: "THE MAGNETIC FIELD IS EARTH'S IMMUNE SYSTEM."

## Physics Concept Teased
Earth's dipole magnetic field deflects the solar wind around the planet — charged particles cannot cross magnetic field lines and must flow around the magnetosphere, with only those channeled along polar field lines entering to create auroras.
