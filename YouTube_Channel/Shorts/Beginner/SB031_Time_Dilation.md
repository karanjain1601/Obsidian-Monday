## Visual Hook (First 3 Seconds)
Two large analog clocks side by side, both starting at exactly 12:00:00. Label left: "STATIONARY." Label right: "MOVING AT 0.99c." A velocity slider shown. Both clocks begin ticking.

## Main Visual Sequence (0:03–0:50)
**Beat 1 (0:03–0:12):** Both clocks ticking. The right clock is visibly slower — its second hand moving at a fraction of the left clock's rate. The difference is immediate and obvious. Velocity: 0.99c.
**Beat 2 (0:12–0:20):** After 60 seconds of real time: left clock reads 1:00:00. Right clock reads approximately 12:08:34. "FOR EVERY 60 SECONDS YOU LIVE, THE TRAVELER AGES 8 SECONDS."
**Beat 3 (0:20–0:28):** Velocity slider moved down to 0.50c. Right clock speeds up — still slower than left, but not as dramatic. Lorentz factor formula shown: "γ = 1/√(1 - v²/c²)."
**Beat 4 (0:28–0:36):** Velocity increased: 0.999c. Right clock nearly frozen — ticking at barely perceptible rate. 0.9999c: the right clock appears completely stopped to the eye.
**Beat 5 (0:36–0:44):** Reset both to 12:00. Let them run for 10 simulated "Earth years." Left clock: 10 years. Right clock: 4.36 years. "THE DIFFERENCE IS PERMANENT. IT IS REAL."
**Beat 6 (0:44–0:50):** "Time doesn't tick the same for everyone." Final: "THIS IS NOT A THOUGHT EXPERIMENT. IT IS MEASURED DAILY."

## Physics Concept Teased
Why does motion through space slow down motion through time — and is this a real physical effect or just a mathematical prediction? (It is real — GPS satellites require time dilation corrections to function.)
