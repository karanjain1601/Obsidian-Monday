## Visual Hook (First 3 Seconds)
A Newton's cradle — 5 shiny balls. Two balls pulled back on the left. Label: "SEQUENTIAL COLLISION RESOLUTION." Released.

## Main Visual Sequence (0:03–0:45)
**Beat 1 (0:03–0:12):** Sequential resolution (wrong): Two balls released. Slow motion. Ball 1 hits Ball 2 — cascade transfer — THREE balls rise on the right instead of two. "WRONG." Label flashes red.
**Beat 2 (0:12–0:20):** Close-up explanation: each collision handled one at a time. Ball 2→3 transfer resolved before Ball 1→2 finalized. Physics processed in wrong order. Phantom momentum.
**Beat 3 (0:20–0:28):** Reset. Label changes: "SIMULTANEOUS COLLISION RESOLUTION." Same two balls released. Slow motion. EXACTLY two balls rise on the right. "CORRECT." Checkmark, green.
**Beat 4 (0:28–0:35):** Side-by-side: "SEQUENTIAL" (3 balls rise) vs "SIMULTANEOUS" (2 balls rise). The only difference: order of operations in the math.
**Beat 5 (0:35–0:40):** "Same physics equations. Same balls. Same everything. Order of operations changes the answer."
**Beat 6 (0:40–0:45):** "ORDER OF OPERATIONS. IN PHYSICS CODE. WHO KNEW?"

## Physics Concept Teased
When multiple simultaneous collisions are resolved sequentially instead of all at once, phantom momentum transfers occur and the result is physically wrong — Newton's cradle becomes Newton's wrong cradle.
