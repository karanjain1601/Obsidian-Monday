## Visual Hook (First 3 Seconds)
A mouse cursor moving across a dark screen. Behind it: fluid dye in three colors swirling in a perfect wake. Not trailing static — fluid dynamics. The wake curls, billows, alive.

## Main Visual Sequence (0:03–0:45)
**Beat 1 (0:03–0:10):** Mouse traces a figure-8. The colored dye follows in a complex, fluid wake — inner loops intersecting and swirling. Color mixing at intersections creates new hues.
**Beat 2 (0:10–0:18):** Mouse stops. Fluid continues evolving — eddies spinning out, vortices cascading smaller and smaller. The fluid has its own momentum. No input needed.
**Beat 3 (0:18–0:26):** FPS counter: 60. "2D NAVIER-STOKES. REAL TIME." The simulation runs at interactive speed on a standard GPU.
**Beat 4 (0:26–0:33):** Vorticity confinement OFF. Eddies soften and blur — smoke loses wispy detail. Toggle ON: sharp vortex structures snap back instantly. Smoke curls with vivid detail again.
**Beat 5 (0:33–0:40):** Mouse draws a slow spiral. Dye wraps into a perfect spiral vortex. Close-up: fluid structure inside the vortex eye — calm center, turbulent edges.
**Beat 6 (0:40–0:45):** Freeze on a stunning fluid art frame. "Navier-Stokes in your browser. What it can't simulate is even wilder."

## Physics Concept Teased
What are the Navier-Stokes equations, and how can they run in real time on a browser canvas while producing smoke-like behavior? What is vorticity confinement?
