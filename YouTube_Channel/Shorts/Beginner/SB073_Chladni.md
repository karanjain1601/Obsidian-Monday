## Visual Hook (First 3 Seconds)
A flat square metal plate covered in fine white sand, uniformly distributed. The sand sits still. Then a vibration begins. The sand begins to move — dancing, bouncing — and then SETTLING into a geometric pattern. Perfect. Unexpected. Beautiful.

## Main Visual Sequence (0:03–0:50)
**Beat 1 (0:03–0:10):** Frequency 1 (lowest mode): sand clears from the center and piles at the edges. Simple, a single line of sand. "f₁ = 220 Hz. MODE (1,1)."
**Beat 2 (0:10–0:18):** Frequency 2 increased. Sand jumps, redistributes. New pattern: a cross — sand in a + shape. "f₂ = 440 Hz. MODE (2,1)."
**Beat 3 (0:18–0:26):** Frequency 3: sand clears to reveal a more complex grid-like pattern. Nodal lines crossing in a more intricate geometry. Each pattern sharp and stable.
**Beat 4 (0:26–0:34):** Higher frequencies: increasingly complex patterns — more nodal lines, finer divisions. Each one a distinct, beautiful standing wave mode of the plate. A rapid succession: 5 distinct patterns.
**Beat 5 (0:34–0:42):** "WHERE DOES THE SAND GO?" Sand accumulates at the NODAL LINES — where the plate doesn't vibrate. Sand is bounced away from antinodes (max vibration) toward nodes (no vibration).
**Beat 6 (0:42–0:50):** Final pattern: the most complex and beautiful, with many nodal lines. "SAND FINDS THE MATHEMATICS. AT EVERY FREQUENCY, A DIFFERENT PATTERN." Final: "CHLADNI PATTERNS. MUSIC MADE VISIBLE."

## Physics Concept Teased
A vibrating plate has specific resonant frequencies at which standing wave patterns form. Sand accumulates at nodal lines (points of zero vibration) and is repelled from antinodes (maximum vibration), making the invisible standing wave modes visible.
