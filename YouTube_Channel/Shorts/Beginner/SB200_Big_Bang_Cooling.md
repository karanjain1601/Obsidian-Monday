## Production Notes
- **Renderer:** Manim for timeline bar and text overlays; Python + Matplotlib for temperature bar and CMB heatmap; Blender for galaxy formation and cosmic web sequence. Video composite in DaVinci Resolve or Premiere.
- **Code complexity:** High overall (multiple distinct scene types). Modular approach: each epoch is a separate animation rendered independently and composited to the master timeline. Temperature bar: animate from 10³² to 2.725 K using log scale on y-axis, color-mapped to blackbody color at each temperature.
- **Key visual trick:** The temperature bar color is set to the actual Planck blackbody color at each temperature — blinding white at t=0, yellow-white at quark epoch, deep orange at recombination, invisible microwave (rendered as false-color purple-blue) at today. This single visual element tells the whole cooling story.
- **Runtime:** 7 distinct epochs × ~5 s each = ~35 s scene time + 5 s hook + 5 s end card = 45 s total. Each epoch must be readable in 5 s — no math equations, labels only.
- **Gotchas:** The scale on the timeline axis must be logarithmic — otherwise the first 3 minutes occupy an invisibly small slice and all history from 1 Gyr onward occupies most of the bar, which is misleading. Use log₁₀(t/1s) as x-axis, clearly labeled. Also: "Big Bang" is NOT an explosion into existing space — the universe expanded from a hot dense state everywhere simultaneously. Avoid showing an outward "explosion" from a point; instead show the SCALE FACTOR increasing uniformly.
