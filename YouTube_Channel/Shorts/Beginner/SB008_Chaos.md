# Short: 0.0001° Makes This Impossible

**Feeds full video:** I Added ONE More Pendulum Rod

---

## Visual Hook (First 3 Seconds)
Two double pendulums side by side, mid-swing, tracing chaotic paths. Then REWIND to t=0. Both frozen. The only text visible: "DIFFERENCE: 0.0001°." Everything else appears identical.

## Main Visual Sequence (0:03–0:50)
**Beat 1 (0:03–0:08):** Release. Both double pendulums swing and tumble chaotically — but identically. Their trails overlay perfectly. "3 SECONDS: TRACKING."
**Beat 2 (0:08–0:14):** At t=5s, a hairline gap appears in the trails. Still overlapping 99% of the time, but diverging. "5 SECONDS: DIVERGING."
**Beat 3 (0:14–0:22):** t=9s. The gap is visible — one pendulum going high while the other goes low. Trails forking. t=12s: completely different configurations. One going left, one going right.
**Beat 4 (0:22–0:30):** t=15s. Full chaos. Both pendulums trace completely different paths that never coincide. The initial overlap shown as a ghost: "THEY STARTED HERE." Two wildly different chaos patterns.
**Beat 5 (0:30–0:38):** Freeze. Zoom in on the starting position. A protractor shows 0.0001° difference. Annotation: "SMALLER THAN THE WIDTH OF A HUMAN HAIR AT THIS SCALE." A pixel-level zoom — they look completely identical at normal view.
**Beat 6 (0:38–0:45):** Time-lapse of total chaos both trails. Text: "SAME LAWS. SAME PHYSICS. UNPREDICTABLE." Final: "THIS IS CHAOS."

## Physics Concept Teased
Why does a tiny — literally sub-pixel — difference in starting angle lead to completely different outcomes within seconds? What does it mean for a system governed by deterministic equations to be impossible to predict?
