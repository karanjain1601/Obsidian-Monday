## Visual Hook (First 3 Seconds)
A golden chain hanging in the air, suspended from one end in a natural catenary curve. It releases — falling, folding, coiling — and lands in a perfect pile on the floor. Clean, physical, satisfying.

## Main Visual Sequence (0:03–0:40)
**Beat 1 (0:03–0:10):** Chain held at one end — dangling portion in a natural curve. Close-up of individual links: physically distinct, articulated. Release.
**Beat 2 (0:10–0:18):** Chain falls. Upper links in free fall, lower links piling. Classic S-curve forming as falling portion's speed exceeds rising pile rate.
**Beat 3 (0:18–0:25):** Close-up of floor impact zone. Individual links arriving, folding, nestling into natural curves. No clipping. No jittering. Physical contact forces.
**Beat 4 (0:25–0:30):** Full chain landed. Natural, organic pile. Camera circles slowly. "ZERO CLIPPING. ZERO TUNNELING. ZERO JITTER."
**Beat 5 (0:30–0:35):** Cut to first attempt: chain phases through the floor, links passing through links, a jittering mess. "WHAT IT LOOKED LIKE ON DAY ONE."
**Beat 6 (0:35–0:40):** Cut back to perfect version. Pile settles completely. "Day 1 vs Day 47. Constraint solving."

## Physics Concept Teased
How do you simulate rigid link constraints in a chain so each link maintains its length and doesn't clip through its neighbors — especially as dozens of links pile up simultaneously?
