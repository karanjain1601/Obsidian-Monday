## Visual Hook (First 3 Seconds)
A phase portrait slowly building — vector field arrows appearing one by one as a neural network is being queried at each point. The portrait is half-built. Beautiful even incomplete.

## Main Visual Sequence (0:03–0:45)
**Beat 1 (0:03–0:10):** The phase portrait completes. Inside the central region: closed ellipses (oscillating pendulums). Outside: open curves winding to the right (rotating pendulums). A golden curve divides them. "THE SEPARATRIX."
**Beat 2 (0:10–0:18):** A trajectory launched from inside the separatrix: it closes into an ellipse — the pendulum oscillates back and forth forever. "OSCILLATING."
**Beat 3 (0:18–0:26):** A trajectory launched from just outside the separatrix: it spirals outward in open curves — the pendulum is doing full rotations. "ROTATING." The same pendulum; different starting energy.
**Beat 4 (0:26–0:32):** A trajectory launched exactly ON the separatrix: it approaches the unstable equilibrium (pendulum straight up) asymptotically — taking infinitely long to reach it. "THE BOUNDARY BETWEEN WORLDS."
**Beat 5 (0:32–0:38):** The training data shown: 500 dots scattered across phase space. The neural network interpolated between them to produce the entire vector field. "LEARNED FROM 500 POINTS."
**Beat 6 (0:38–0:45):** Final: the separatrix glowing gold, the two regions clearly delineated. "OSCILLATING. ROTATING. SEPARATED BY ONE CURVE." Final: "A NEURAL NET DREW THE SOUL OF A PENDULUM."

## Physics Concept Teased
The separatrix in a pendulum's phase portrait is the boundary between finite oscillation and infinite rotation — it passes through the unstable equilibrium and represents the set of initial conditions where the pendulum has exactly the energy to reach the top.
