# Short: Accretion Disk Wraps Around

**Feeds full video:** I Rendered the Most Beautiful Thing in the Universe

---

## Visual Hook (First 3 Seconds)
Absolute black. Then: a black hole silhouette appears at center — the shadow surrounded by a faint photon ring. Nothing else. Perfect simplicity. Ominous. The universe's most powerful vacuum.

## Main Visual Sequence (0:03–0:55)
**Beat 1 (0:03–0:13):** The accretion disk is added: a flat ring of glowing plasma around the equator. No GR lensing yet. It looks like a vinyl record floating in space. Flat, simple.
**Beat 2 (0:13–0:23):** GR lensing enabled. Instantly the image transforms — the far side of the disk, which should be behind the black hole, appears bent over the top of it. The disk now looks like it wraps over the black hole. "GRAVITY BENDS THE IMAGE."
**Beat 3 (0:23–0:33):** Doppler shift enabled. The left side (approaching) blazes brighter and bluer. The right side (receding) dims and reddens. One side of the disk burns twice as bright as the other.
**Beat 4 (0:33–0:43):** Photon ring sharpens — a thin bright ring at the innermost stable orbit. Inner shadow: perfectly black. The full Interstellar-style image is now complete.
**Beat 5 (0:43–0:50):** Camera slowly tilts to view the disk from the equatorial plane. The image morphs as the viewing angle changes — the top-disk structure giving way to a face-on ring view.
**Beat 6 (0:50–0:55):** Freeze on the most beautiful render. "GENERAL RELATIVITY. RAY TRACING. ONE RENDER." Final: "THIS TOOK 2 BILLION RAYS."

## Physics Concept Teased
Why does the far side of the accretion disk appear above the black hole instead of behind it, why does one side blaze twice as bright, and how can a single rendering algorithm produce all of this from first principles?
