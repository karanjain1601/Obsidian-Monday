## Visual Hook (First 3 Seconds)
A kite flying steadily against a blue sky. It's not moving. It's in perfect equilibrium. Then four force arrows appear simultaneously: lift (up-forward), drag (backward-down), weight (straight down), string tension (down-backward). "FOUR FORCES. ONE BALANCE."

## Main Visual Sequence (0:03–0:40)
**Beat 1 (0:03–0:10):** The four forces shown as vectors on the kite. String tension shown pulling down and backward toward the person's hand. Kite stationary. Vector sum: zero. "NET FORCE = 0. EQUILIBRIUM."
**Beat 2 (0:10–0:18):** String length increased (person lets out string): kite angle changes, lift and drag ratio shifts. Kite climbs — new equilibrium found at a different altitude. "LONGER STRING: KITE CLIMBS."
**Beat 3 (0:18–0:25):** Wind speed increases. Drag and lift both increase. The kite pulls harder on the string. Force balance reestablished. "MORE WIND: STRONGER PULL."
**Beat 4 (0:25–0:32):** Kite angle of attack shown: optimal angle gives maximum lift-to-drag ratio. Too steep: stall, kite tumbles. Too shallow: sinks. "THE KITE FINDS ITS OWN ANGLE."
**Beat 5 (0:32–0:37):** The string is the key: it constrains the kite to a specific locus of possible equilibrium positions. Without the string: the kite would blow away. The string provides the fourth force.
**Beat 6 (0:37–0:40):** Final four-force diagram. "WIND. STRING. GRAVITY. SHAPE. THE KITE BALANCES THEM ALL." Final: "EQUILIBRIUM IN THE AIR."

## Physics Concept Teased
A kite reaches equilibrium when lift (perpendicular to relative wind), drag (along relative wind), weight (down), and string tension (toward the holder) all cancel out — the string constrains the equilibrium position while the kite's shape determines what equilibrium is possible.
