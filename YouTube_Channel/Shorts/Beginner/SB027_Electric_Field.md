## Visual Hook (First 3 Seconds)
A positive charge (red sphere, + symbol) and a negative charge (blue sphere, - symbol) at opposite sides of the frame. Between them: electric field lines in the classic dipole pattern. Clean, textbook-perfect.

## Main Visual Sequence (0:03–0:45)
**Beat 1 (0:03–0:10):** Static dipole field. Lines flowing from + to -. Equipotential rings around each charge. Beautiful and familiar.
**Beat 2 (0:10–0:18):** Negative charge dragged slowly toward positive. Field lines warp in real time — bending and reconnecting as geometry changes. No lag. Instant update.
**Beat 3 (0:18–0:26):** Charges very close: field lines bunch tightly between them, concentrated. Equipotentials compress. Field strongest where lines are densest.
**Beat 4 (0:26–0:32):** Third charge added (another positive). Field lines reconfigure immediately — repulsion visible as lines deflect away from each other.
**Beat 5 (0:32–0:38):** Third charge dragged to different positions — a complex three-body electrostatic field in motion. Lines always point + to -, always perpendicular to equipotentials.
**Beat 6 (0:38–0:45):** Freeze on a beautiful three-charge field configuration. "ELECTRIC FIELD. LIVE. INTERACTIVE." Final: "Every point in space has a direction and a strength."

## Physics Concept Teased
Electric field lines are a visualization of the vector field pervading all space around charges — every point has a specific direction and magnitude determined by Coulomb's law from every charge simultaneously.
