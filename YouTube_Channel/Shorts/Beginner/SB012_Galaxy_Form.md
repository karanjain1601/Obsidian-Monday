## Visual Hook (First 3 Seconds)
10,000 white dots scattered randomly against a black background. Text: "10,000 RANDOM PARTICLES." Then: "ONE FORCE: GRAVITY." They begin to move.

## Main Visual Sequence (0:03–0:55)
**Beat 1 (0:03–0:13):** Particles drift slowly toward local density concentrations. Small clumps forming. Nothing dramatic yet — just a slow gathering. "50 MILLION SIMULATED YEARS."
**Beat 2 (0:13–0:23):** Several large clumps growing, pulling in surrounding particles. Filamentary structure appearing between them. Random scatter becoming organized. Particle counter holds at 10,000.
**Beat 3 (0:23–0:33):** One clump dominates — growing into a central mass. Other clumps drawn toward it, some merging. A rotation beginning — one side slightly faster than the other.
**Beat 4 (0:33–0:43):** Rotation becomes undeniable. Central mass surrounded by a rotating disk. Spiral arms beginning to form as angular momentum organizes the system.
**Beat 5 (0:43–0:50):** Fully formed spiral structure. A bright dense core. Two loose spiral arms. Individual star points visible in the arms. Self-organized from pure gravity.
**Beat 6 (0:50–0:55):** Freeze. "NO TEMPLATE. NO INSTRUCTIONS. JUST GRAVITY." Counter: "500 MILLION SIMULATED YEARS."

## Physics Concept Teased
How does pure gravitational attraction between identical particles, with no preferred direction or template, spontaneously organize random matter into a rotating spiral galaxy?
