# Short: Springs Trading Energy

**Feeds full video:** When Springs Share Masses, Something Magical Happens
**Duration:** ~50 seconds

---

## Visual Hook (First 3 Seconds)
Two pendulums hanging side by side. Left one: swinging fully, wide arc. Right one: completely motionless. A thin coupling spring connects them at mid-height. Text: "NOBODY TOUCHED THE RIGHT ONE."

## Main Visual Sequence (0:03–0:50)
**Beat 1 (0:03–0:12):** Energy bars appear for each pendulum. Left: 100%. Right: 0%. Left swings fully. Right is still. Coupling spring barely twitches with each swing.
**Beat 2 (0:12–0:22):** Left pendulum begins to diminish — barely at first. Right one begins a tiny trembling motion. Energy numbers: Left 70%, Right 30%. The transfer is happening.
**Beat 3 (0:22–0:30):** Midpoint: Left 50%, Right 50%. Both pendulums at equal amplitudes. Coupling spring taut. Beautiful symmetry for exactly one swing.
**Beat 4 (0:30–0:38):** Left diminishing fast. Right growing. Left 20%, Right 80%. Right now swinging nearly as wide as left originally was.
**Beat 5 (0:38–0:44):** Complete transfer. Left: 0% — completely still. Right: 100% — swinging the full original amplitude. "THE ENERGY IS GONE. AND GONE AGAIN." Because now the right starts transferring back.
**Beat 6 (0:44–0:50):** Time-lapse shows the sloshing continuing: full transfer, back, full transfer, back, rhythmically. "COMPLETE TRANSFER EVERY 8 SECONDS." Final: "WHERE DOES IT GO?"

## Physics Concept Teased
Coupled oscillators exchange energy through shared normal modes — the energy sloshes back and forth completely at a rate determined by how tightly they are coupled, not by how fast they swing individually.
