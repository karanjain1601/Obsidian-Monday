# Short: The Younger Twin Returns

**Feeds full video:** The Twin Paradox Has a Definite Answer

## Visual Hook (First 3 Seconds)
Two identical avatars side by side: TWIN A (stays on Earth) and TWIN B (in a spaceship). Two odometers below each: "EARTH TIME: 0.00y" and "TRAVELER TIME: 0.00y." Spaceship launches.

## Main Visual Sequence (0:03–0:50)
**Beat 1 (0:03–0:12):** Spaceship launches at 0.9c. Both odometers tick. Earth time ticks fast. Traveler time ticks slower — visibly, measurably slower. Earth: 2 years. Traveler: 0.87 years.
**Beat 2 (0:12–0:20):** Spaceship reaches its destination. Earth time: 5 years. Traveler time: 2.18 years. Spaceship turns around. The turnaround is shown as a brief deceleration and reacceleration.
**Beat 3 (0:20–0:28):** Return journey. Earth time accelerating past traveler time. Earth: 8 years. Traveler: 3.48 years. The gap is widening.
**Beat 4 (0:28–0:36):** Spaceship returns. Earth time: 10.00 years. Traveler time: 4.36 years. The two avatars stand together again. Twin A has aged 10 years. Twin B has aged 4.36 years. Both counters freeze.
**Beat 5 (0:36–0:43):** "WHO IS OLDER?" Answer label: Twin A visibly older (gray hair, tired). Twin B: younger. "THE TRAVELER IS YOUNGER. PERMANENTLY. BY 5.64 YEARS."
**Beat 6 (0:43–0:50):** "Relativity doesn't make them symmetric. The turnaround breaks the symmetry." Final: "THE TWIN PARADOX HAS A DEFINITE ANSWER."

## Physics Concept Teased
The twin paradox seems symmetric — each twin sees the other's clock running slow — but it isn't, because only one twin accelerates during the turnaround. The traveler is genuinely, permanently younger.
