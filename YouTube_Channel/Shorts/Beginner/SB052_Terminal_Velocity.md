# Short: Terminal Velocity

**Feeds full video:** Why Skydivers Stop Accelerating
**Duration:** ~45 seconds

---

## Visual Hook (First 3 Seconds)
A skydiver silhouette against a blue sky, falling. A velocity vs. time graph on the right side. The velocity line is rising linearly — then it begins to curve. Then it flattens completely. "WHY DID IT STOP?"

## Main Visual Sequence (0:03–0:45)
**Beat 1 (0:03–0:10):** No drag: velocity graph is a straight line rising at 9.8 m/s². "JUST GRAVITY. NO AIR. FOREVER ACCELERATING." Skydiver accelerating infinitely. Dashed line extrapolating to impossible speeds.
**Beat 2 (0:10–0:20):** With drag: velocity curve shown. Rising steeply at first (drag small when slow), then curving, decelerating, approaching a flat asymptote. "DRAG IS PROPORTIONAL TO v²."
**Beat 3 (0:20–0:28):** At terminal velocity: two force arrows shown on the skydiver — gravity (down, constant) and drag (up, growing). At terminal velocity: they're equal length. Acceleration = 0.
**Beat 4 (0:28–0:35):** Terminal velocity formula: "v_t = √(2mg/ρCdA)." Variables labeled: mass (m), gravity (g), air density (ρ), drag coefficient (Cd), cross-section area (A). Skydiver flattening their body: Cd and A change, v_t changes.
**Beat 5 (0:35–0:40):** Comparison: skydiver belly-down (large A) → low terminal velocity ~54 m/s. Skydiver diving head-first (small A) → high terminal velocity ~89 m/s. Same person, same gravity, different shape.
**Beat 6 (0:40–0:45):** Graph frozen at terminal velocity. "GRAVITY NEVER STOPS PULLING. DRAG NEVER STOPS PUSHING. THEY AGREE TO DISAGREE." Final: "54 m/s. FOREVER."

## Physics Concept Teased
Terminal velocity isn't where gravity stops working — it's where drag force has grown (as v² increases) to exactly balance gravity. At that point, net force is zero and acceleration stops, so velocity is constant forever.
