## Visual Hook (First 3 Seconds)
A dark thundercloud. From the bottom: a faint, branching structure grows downward — the stepped leader. Each branch is random, probabilistic. Growing, splitting, reaching toward the ground. Then: a bright upward streamer from a tree. They connect.

## Main Visual Sequence (0:03–0:45)
**Beat 1 (0:03–0:10):** The cloud base: a negatively charged region. Electric field visualization shows field lines pointing downward. The stepped leader: a plasma channel propagating in discrete 50m steps, each step taking ~1 ms. "50 METER STEPS. 1 MILLISECOND EACH."
**Beat 2 (0:10–0:18):** The branching is shown as Laplacian random growth — the leader grows preferentially where the electric field is strongest, which is at the tip of the existing channel. "GROWS WHERE THE FIELD IS STRONGEST."
**Beat 3 (0:18–0:25):** Multiple branches visible — the leader is NOT a single channel but many competing branches, each randomly extending. The longest branch wins (reaches ground first).
**Beat 4 (0:25–0:32):** Upward streamers from the ground (tall objects: trees, towers, wet soil). When a downward branch and upward streamer meet: a complete conductive path is formed. "CONNECTION."
**Beat 5 (0:32–0:38):** The RETURN STROKE: 200 million volts, 30,000 amps, traveling UPWARD along the connected path at 100,000 km/s. The bright flash we see is the return stroke — not the leader. Slow-motion return stroke shown.
**Beat 6 (0:38–0:45):** "The lightning leader is invisible. The bright flash travels UPWARD. You see the return stroke, not the leader." Final: "LIGHTNING GOES UP. YOUR EYES LIED."

## Physics Concept Teased
A lightning bolt is first an invisible stepped leader growing downward, then a return stroke traveling upward along the completed path — the bright flash we see is the upward return stroke, not the downward leader.
