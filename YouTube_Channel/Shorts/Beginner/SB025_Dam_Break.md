## Visual Hook (First 3 Seconds)
A wall of blue particles packed tightly on the left — 10,000 of them. A thin black wall holds them back. Particles jostling. Counter: "10,000 PARTICLES." Then the wall vanishes.

## Main Visual Sequence (0:03–0:50)
**Beat 1 (0:03–0:08):** Dam breaks. Slow-motion: the particle mass begins to fall and rush right. Individual particles visible — each a smooth blue sphere pressing against neighbors.
**Beat 2 (0:08–0:18):** Wave front rushes across the floor. Leading-edge particles with no neighbors ahead accelerate fastest. Wave front steeper, faster. Splash zone forming.
**Beat 3 (0:18–0:28):** Deep particle mass creates pressure — SPH pressure forces pushing particles apart enough to prevent compression. Close-up: individual particle interactions visible.
**Beat 4 (0:28–0:36):** Wave hits the right wall. SPLASH in slow motion. Particles climbing the wall, reaching peak height. Individual droplets arcing and falling back.
**Beat 5 (0:36–0:43):** Fluid settles — particles finding equilibrium. Sloshing back and forth with diminishing amplitude. "10,000 PARTICLES. 60 FPS."
**Beat 6 (0:43–0:50):** Final settled state. "Each particle knows only its neighbors. Together — a wave." Final: "SPH: SMOOTHED PARTICLE HYDRODYNAMICS."

## Physics Concept Teased
How can smoothed particle hydrodynamics create realistic fluid behavior from 10,000 independent particles that each only communicate with their nearest neighbors — no grid, no fluid equations directly?
