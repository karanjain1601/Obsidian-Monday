## Visual Hook (First 3 Seconds)
A see-saw. A 100 kg person (labeled "100 kg") on the left at 1 meter from the center. Right side: empty. See-saw tilts left immediately. Text: "HOW DO YOU BALANCE THIS?"

## Main Visual Sequence (0:03–0:40)
**Beat 1 (0:03–0:08):** Unbalanced: 100 kg at 1m left. See-saw tilted all the way left. Torque arrow (counterclockwise): "τ = 100 × 1 = 100 N·m CCW." Right side torque: 0.
**Beat 2 (0:08–0:16):** A question mark person appears on the right at 2m. "WHAT MASS BALANCES THIS?" The see-saw tips slightly right, then adjusts. Balance equation: "m × 2m = 100kg × 1m."
**Beat 3 (0:16–0:24):** Answer appears: "m = 50 kg." A 50 kg figure appears at 2m. See-saw levels perfectly. Both torque arrows shown: 100 N·m CW = 100 N·m CCW. Balanced. Satisfying.
**Beat 4 (0:24–0:30):** Move the 50 kg person to 1m. See-saw tips left (50 kg at 1m vs 100 kg at 1m — still unbalanced). Move to 2m: balanced. "SAME PERSON, DIFFERENT DISTANCE, DIFFERENT TORQUE."
**Beat 5 (0:30–0:35):** The leverage principle: a 10 kg person at 10m balances the 100 kg person at 1m. Same principle — extreme leverage. "TORQUE = FORCE × DISTANCE."
**Beat 6 (0:35–0:40):** Archimedes quote: "Give me a lever long enough and a place to stand, and I will move the world." Final: "TORQUE = FORCE × ARM. THAT'S ALL IT IS."

## Physics Concept Teased
Torque (τ = r × F) means that a small force applied at a large distance creates the same rotational effect as a large force at a small distance — the distance from the pivot is just as important as the force magnitude.

## On-Screen Text / Captions
- "HOW DO YOU BALANCE THIS?" (opening)
- Torque equations: "τ = F × r = 100 × 1 = 100 N·m" (both sides)
- "m = 50 kg." (answer, Beat 3)
- "TORQUE = FORCE × DISTANCE." (Beat 5)
- "TORQUE = FORCE × ARM. THAT'S ALL IT IS." (final)
