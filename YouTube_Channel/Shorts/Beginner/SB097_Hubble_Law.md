# Short: Hubble Recession

**Feeds full video:** The Expanding Universe
**Duration:** ~45 seconds

---

## Visual Hook (First 3 Seconds)
A grid of dots — galaxies — distributed uniformly. They all begin moving. Moving AWAY from everything else. There's no center — every dot is moving away from every other dot simultaneously. "EVERYTHING IS MOVING AWAY FROM EVERYTHING ELSE."

## Main Visual Sequence (0:03–0:45)
**Beat 1 (0:03–0:10):** The grid expands. An observer dot (us) is marked. From our perspective: all other dots move away. Arrows showing recession velocities — proportional to distance. "FARTHER = FASTER."
**Beat 2 (0:10–0:18):** Hubble's law plotted live: velocity vs distance. All galaxies fall on a straight line through the origin. Slope = H₀ (Hubble constant). "v = H₀ × d. HUBBLE'S LAW."
**Beat 3 (0:18–0:25):** Choose any OTHER dot as the observer: exactly the same picture. All dots receding from it, at the same rate. "EVERY GALAXY SEES THE SAME THING. THERE IS NO CENTER."
**Beat 4 (0:25–0:32):** "Is everything moving away from us specifically?" Rewind the expansion. All galaxies converging. 13.8 billion years ago: a point. "THE BIG BANG. THE UNIVERSE BEGAN SMALL."
**Beat 5 (0:32–0:38):** Cosmic scale factor shown: a(t) plot. At Big Bang: a = 0. Now: a = 1. In the future: a > 1, increasing faster (dark energy). "THE EXPANSION IS ACCELERATING."
**Beat 6 (0:38–0:45):** "Some galaxies are receding faster than light. We'll never see them." Observable universe boundary shown. Final: "THE UNIVERSE IS BIGGER THAN WHAT WE CAN SEE."

## Physics Concept Teased
The Hubble law (v = H₀d) means recession velocity is proportional to distance — but this is not objects moving through space, it is space itself expanding, so there is no center of expansion and distant galaxies can recede faster than light.
