## Visual Hook (First 3 Seconds)
A grid of tiny arrows — the Ising model. High temperature. The arrows are completely random — half up, half down, pointing in every direction, chaotic. Temperature: "T = 10." No order.

## Main Visual Sequence (0:03–0:50)
**Beat 1 (0:03–0:10):** Temperature high — chaotic arrow arrangement. Magnetization shown: "M ≈ 0.00." Occasional small domains forming and dissolving instantly. Nothing persists.
**Beat 2 (0:10–0:20):** Temperature slider slowly decreasing. "T = 7." Slightly larger fleeting domains. "T = 5." Bigger domains, lasting longer. The arrows are beginning to coordinate. Magnetization: "M = 0.12."
**Beat 3 (0:20–0:28):** Temperature approaching the critical point: "T = 2.27 (Tc)." Large domains spanning the entire grid appear and disappear in waves. Critical fluctuations on all length scales simultaneously. "CRITICAL POINT."
**Beat 4 (0:28–0:35):** Temperature drops just below Tc. INSTANT TRANSFORMATION. Large domains SNAP into existence and lock in. The grid is now mostly one color. Magnetization: "M = 0.85." A magnet was born.
**Beat 5 (0:35–0:42):** Temperature raised back above Tc. Domains immediately dissolve. Magnetization crashes to 0. Raised again: instant order returns. The phase transition is sharp, reversible, dramatic.
**Beat 6 (0:42–0:50):** Frozen at the phase transition. Half-up, half-down, critical domains. "This is how magnets form. One degree changes everything." Final: "PHASE TRANSITION."

## Physics Concept Teased
The Ising model shows a phase transition — at the Curie temperature, random thermal fluctuations suddenly cannot overcome ferromagnetic alignment, and large-scale magnetic order emerges spontaneously from pure local interactions.

## Production Notes
Aspect ratio: 9:16. Frame rate: 60fps. Grid: at least 128×128 spins. Spin up: blue pixels. Spin down: red pixels. Arrow display optional — colored pixels are clearer at this scale. Temperature slider prominent. Magnetization bar shown live. The snap at Tc must be visually dramatic — the grid should appear to instantly fill with one dominant color.
