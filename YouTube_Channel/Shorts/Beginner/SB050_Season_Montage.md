## Audio
A sweeping orchestral piece that builds through the montage. Each cut adds another instrument. By the black hole: full orchestra. Then at the final bouncing ball: stripped back to a single piano note. The ending is quiet, not triumphant — the beginning of the journey, not the end.

## Production Notes
Aspect ratio: 9:16. Frame rate: 60fps. Each cut should be exactly 6 seconds — tight, rhythmic, confident. Each clip: the single most visually striking moment from that simulation. Color grade: each clip retains its original color palette — the diversity of visual styles is part of the statement. The montage must flow — use cross-dissolve transitions, not hard cuts. End on the bouncing ball: it looks different now that you know where it leads.
