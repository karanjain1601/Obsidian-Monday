## Main Visual Sequence (0:03–0:50)

**0:10–0:18** — The Calabi-Yau manifold structure: the 6-real-dimensional internal space X₆ must be Kähler (complex manifold with Hermitian metric) and Ricci-flat (Rμν = 0). The holonomy group SU(3) is labeled. The Euler characteristic χ = 2(h¹¹ − h²¹) = 2(3−243) = −480 is computed and displayed in gold.

**0:18–0:26** — Harmonic forms on the CY: (1,1)-forms ωᵢ (shown as colored 2-surfaces within the manifold) give rise to vector multiplets in 4D. **0:26–0:34** — The 4D effective theory: after compactifying M-theory on CY₃, the 4D N=2 supergravity theory has gauge group with rank h¹¹ = 3. The vector multiplet moduli space is a Special Kähler manifold. A schematic of the moduli space (triangle with three vertices = three vector moduli) is shown, with a metric g_ij displayed as a 3×3 matrix.

**0:34–0:42** — The G₂ manifold compactification for 4D N=1: M-theory on a G₂ holonomy 7-manifold gives the more phenomenologically interesting N=1 case. A G₂ manifold cross-section (associative 3-cycle highlighted in cyan) is shown. The ADE singularity structure produces non-Abelian gauge symmetry: a conical singularity labeled "A₂ → SU(3)."

**0:42–0:50** — Final: the 11D spacetime splits as M⁴ × CY₃ — shown as a Cartesian product of a flat 4D Minkowski space (grid) and the compact CY manifold (tiny torus icon). Length scale: "CY radius ~ l_Planck." Fade to CodedLaws logo.

## Physics Concept Teased
M-theory compactifications on Calabi-Yau threefolds produce 4-dimensional supergravity theories whose field content is determined by the topological invariants (Hodge numbers) of the CY manifold. The number of moduli fields, gauge groups, and generations of matter all encode the CY geometry.
