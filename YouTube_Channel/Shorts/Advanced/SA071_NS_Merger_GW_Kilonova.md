## Visual Hook (First 3 Seconds)
Two bright white neutron stars spiral together in a tight orbit. The gravitational wave strain (a sine chirp) is shown as a gold waveform below. They collide — a blinding white flash fills the screen. Then: an expanding orange/red fireball grows outward labeled "Kilonova." Multi-colored jets (blue-white) shoot from the poles. Text: "GW170817 — Aug 17, 2017."

## Main Visual Sequence (0:03–0:50)
**0:03–0:10** — The GW170817 inspiral phase: two NS (masses 1.17 M_☉ and 1.36 M_☉) shown spiraling together. The chirp signal frequency sweeps from 24 Hz to 2 kHz in 100 seconds. Below: the strain h(t) from LIGO Hanford (red), Livingston (blue), and Virgo (green) all agree. Localization: 28 deg² sky area shown on a star map.

**0:10–0:18** — The merger itself at t = 0: a relativistic MHD simulation snapshot — a central dense remnant (white hot, ρ = 10¹⁷ kg/m³) surrounded by a tidal debris disk (orange, T = 10¹⁰ K) extending to 50 km. A short gamma-ray burst (GRB 170817A) is detected 1.74 seconds after merger — shown as a gold burst from the polar jet. "First simultaneous GW + GRB detection."

**0:18–0:26** — The kilonova AT2017gfo light curve: optical brightness (AB magnitude) vs time (days post-merger). The kilonova peaks at 17.1 magnitude in the visual band 12 hours after merger (gold curve), then quickly reddens — blue component (lanthanide-poor, T = 8000 K, blue curve) plus red component (lanthanide-rich, T = 2500 K, red curve). The red component shows strontium (Sr) absorption lines.

**0:26–0:34** — r-process nucleosynthesis: neutron-rich ejecta (shown as gold dots streaming outward from merger) undergoes rapid neutron capture. A nuclear chart (Z vs N, colored by binding energy) shows the r-process path — a zigzag line running along the neutron-rich edge, far from stability. Gold (Z=79), platinum (Z=78), and strontium (Z=38) are highlighted in their colors. "0.005 M_☉ of r-process material ejected."

**0:34–0:42** — Hubble constant measurement from GW170817: the GW amplitude gives luminosity distance D_L = 40 ± 8 Mpc. The host galaxy recession velocity v_host = 3017 km/s (from redshift). H_0 = v/D = 70 ± 12 km/s/Mpc shown in gold. This is the first "standard siren" measurement — a gravitational wave version of the cosmic distance ladder.

**0:42–0:50** — Summary: the multi-messenger timeline displayed as a horizontal bar chart. t = −100 s: GW chirp begins. t = 0: merger. t = +1.74 s: GRB. t = +11 hr: kilonova peaks. t = +9 days: X-ray jet. t = +16 days: radio counterpart. "The most observed event in GW astronomy." Fade to CodedLaws logo.

## Physics Concept Teased
GW170817 was the first neutron star merger observed in both gravitational waves and electromagnetic radiation. The merger produced a short gamma-ray burst, a kilonova powered by r-process nucleosynthesis, and a measurement of the Hubble constant — demonstrating the power of multi-messenger gravitational wave astronomy.
