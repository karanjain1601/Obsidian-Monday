## Visual Hook (First 3 Seconds)
An astronaut (silhouette in white) approaches a black hole event horizon (glowing red ring). Two possible fates shown as a split screen: left = smooth horizon (astronaut passes through, "No drama"), right = "FIREWALL" (a blazing wall of red/orange/yellow flame at the horizon, astronaut vaporizes instantly). Text: "AMPS paradox, 2012."

## Main Visual Sequence (0:03–0:50)
**0:03–0:10** — The three pillars of physics in conflict: (1) Equivalence principle: horizon is locally flat for infalling observer. (2) Unitarity: information escapes in Hawking radiation. (3) Monogamy of entanglement: one qubit cannot be entangled with two partners. Each pillar is shown as a colored column — cyan, gold, magenta — supporting a roof labeled "Consistent Physics."

**0:10–0:18** — The monogamy problem: a late-time Hawking photon B (gold) must be maximally entangled with early-time radiation E (cyan) for unitarity. But equivalence principle requires B also be entangled with its partner A inside the horizon (magenta). Three-party entanglement shown as a triangle: B−E (cyan line) and B−A (magenta line) both at maximum. Monogamy forbids this: "|B,E⟩_max + |B,A⟩_max → CONTRADICTION" in red.

**0:18–0:26** — The firewall: to preserve unitarity + monogamy, the B−A entanglement must be severed at the horizon. This rupture of entanglement releases enormous energy — the "firewall." A visual of the entanglement string being cut: a scissor icon cuts the magenta B−A line, and a burst of red/orange energy erupts at the horizon. "T_firewall ~ T_Planck ~ 10³² K."

**0:26–0:34** — Proposed resolutions: three icons appear in a row. (1) ER=EPR (Maldacena-Susskind): entanglement = wormhole — the entangled pairs B and A are connected by a Planck-scale ER bridge (Einstein-Rosen bridge shown as a tiny tube). (2) Fuzzballs (string theory): the horizon is replaced by a stringy fuzzball — no interior, no paradox. (3) Soft hair (Hawking-Perry-Strominger): infrared gauge symmetry stores information in "soft hair" on the horizon.

**0:34–0:42** — The black hole complementarity picture: an outside observer (red figure) sees the infalling observer (blue figure) burn up at the horizon. The infalling observer (blue frame) sees nothing special. Both descriptions are correct but cannot be compared without violating causality. A causal diamond diagram shows the two descriptions in non-overlapping spacetime regions.

**0:42–0:50** — Summary diagram: the three AMPS assumptions as checkboxes — (1) Equivalence principle ✓, (2) Unitarity ✓, (3) Effective field theory ✓. Then: "All three cannot be simultaneously true." One box gets an X — but which one? "Open problem." Fade to CodedLaws logo.

## Physics Concept Teased
The AMPS firewall paradox shows that three foundational principles — the equivalence principle, unitarity of quantum mechanics, and effective field theory below the Planck scale — cannot simultaneously hold for old (post-Page-time) black holes. At least one must fail, and physics has not yet determined which.
